A complete kit

my vehicle is where I have my largest kit. Sometimes that has been designed to supply everything I need for 2 weeks. Currently I have decided I only need it to supply me for 3-5 days. I keep enough food in my pantry for over a month. I have water stored at the house. it is not drinkable but it is chemical free so I just need a live straw to make it drinkable.

Being prepared starts right when you wakeup in the morning. People take a shower to prepare not to stink. We put on fresh closes to prepare to look good. So the first thing I am going to list here is what I carry on my person every day. Preparing for a normal day as an office person. If you are someone that actually works for a living this might include the equipment you need to do your job. Steel tow boots, leather gloves, a belt, ect..

Many things have multiple uses, so you will see the same item sometimes included in multiple places.
